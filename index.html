About us

Who we are

We are the graduate workers, teaching and research assistants, postdoctoral researchers, and academic staff who keep Stevens running: teaching the classes, running the labs, and producing the research the university is known for.

Right now, decisions about our pay, healthcare, workloads, and protections are made for us, not with us. When we form a union, those decisions become something we negotiate, together, as equals, in a contract the university is legally bound to honor. That's the difference between a promise and a guarantee.

This is about partnership, not conflict. The first step is simple: a majority of us sign union cards, and we ask Stevens to recognize our shared voice.

  • What a union is All of us, organized, bargaining one contract together, instead of negotiating alone.
  • Who it's for Grad workers, TAs, RAs, postdocs, and research staff across every department.
  • How it happens Most of us sign cards → we ask for recognition or hold an election → we negotiate a contract together.
  • Who runs it We do. Workers make every decision democratically, from the bottom up.

Why we're organizing

What we're working toward

These are the things that matter most to us, and a contract lets us put them in writing, together.

💵

Fair, livable pay

Stipends and wages that keep pace with the cost of living in the area, with raises we can plan around.

➕

Real healthcare & benefits

Affordable, comprehensive health, dental, and mental-health coverage, plus fee relief and family support.

🛡️

Job security

Clear appointments, transparent workloads, and fair notice when things change.

🌍

Support for international colleagues

Extra support for colleagues whose visas depend on their jobs, so status never has to be a worry at work.

🔬

Stable research funding

Predictable, guaranteed funding so a gap between grants doesn't derail a degree or a career.

⚖️

Respect & a fair process

A clear, fair way to raise concerns, and real protections against harassment and discrimination.
