Sign your union card

Signing a card is how we show there's real, broad support for a union. It says: "I want a union, and I authorize SAW to represent me." It is confidential, it costs nothing, and it does not commit you to dues today.

  1. 1A majority of us sign cards.
  2. 2We ask Stevens for recognition, or hold an election.
  3. 3We bargain our first contract, together.

🔒 Confidential. Your card is seen only by the worker organizing committee. Use a personal email, not your Stevens address.
