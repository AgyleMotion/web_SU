International Workers & Research Funding

Our union is stronger because it's global

International researchers are the backbone of academic work at Stevens, and they shoulder risks their domestic colleagues never have to think about. A union contract is one of the most powerful tools to protect them.

Your status shouldn't be a bargaining chip

When a visa is tied to a job, losing the job can mean losing the right to stay in the country. That imbalance can silence people. Contract language (just cause for discipline, notice periods, and a neutral grievance process) makes it far harder for that power to be misused.

Funding you can plan a life around

International workers often can't take outside jobs to cover a funding gap. We're organizing for guaranteed, multi-year funding commitments, protected stipends during grant transitions, and support for the fees and costs like SEVIS, visa renewals, and travel that fall hardest on international colleagues.

Support that travels with you

From summer funding to emergency hardship funds to clear policies on remote work and travel, a contract turns "talk to your advisor" into enforceable rights that don't depend on who your PI is or where you're from.

Is it safe for international workers to sign a card? Yes. Federal labor law protects your right to organize regardless of immigration status, and your card is confidential. More in the FAQ →
