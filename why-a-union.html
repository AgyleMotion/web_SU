Why We're Organizing

What we're fighting for

These aren't favors to ask for. They're terms to negotiate. A union contract lets us win and protect them in writing.

💵

Fair, livable pay

Stipends and wages that keep pace with the cost of living in the area, with raises we can count on, not hope for.

➕

Real healthcare & benefits

Affordable, comprehensive health, dental, and mental-health coverage, plus fee relief and family support.

🛡️

Job security

Clear appointments, transparent workloads, and protection from being cut loose without cause or notice.

🌍

International worker protections

Safeguards for colleagues whose visas depend on their jobs, so immigration status is never used as leverage.

🔬

Stable research funding

Predictable, guaranteed funding so a grant gap or an advisor's whim can't derail a degree or a career.

⚖️

Respect & a fair process

A real grievance procedure and strong protections against harassment, discrimination, and retaliation.
